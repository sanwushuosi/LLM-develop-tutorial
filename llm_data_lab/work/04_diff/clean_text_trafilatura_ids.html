The South Carolina Masonic Research Society President message:

Brothers,
I hope this newsletter finds you in continued health and good spirits.
We are approaching our third quarterly meeting of the year and I find my self wondering where the year has gone so fast. The Officers and I are continuing to work hard on behalf of the Society and the year has gone
