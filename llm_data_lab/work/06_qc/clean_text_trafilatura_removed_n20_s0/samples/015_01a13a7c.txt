| What Does Influence Really Mean? Influence is often mistaken for popularity.  In professional settings, it’s something quite different.  It’s about being trusted.  It’s about being the person people quote when you’re not in the room, recommend when you’re not watching, and include in conversations because your insight makes things clearer.  Your influence network isn’t always large.  But it’s pivotal.  It includes mentors, collaborators, peers, industry leaders and decision-makers who respect your voice.  It’s a network built on mutual trust and professional substance. |  |
