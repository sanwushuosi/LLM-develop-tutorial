We are honored to receive a $10,066 grant from Firehouse Subs, Firehouse Subs Public Safety Foundation. The funds will go directly towards the purchase of Seven Zoll AED Plus & Accessories, fulfilling a critical need within our department. Thank you to Firehouse Subs Public Safety Foundation, our local Firehouse Subs restaurant in Charleston and the generous donors who made this possible. This equipment will greatly enhance our department's ability to saving a life in the event of someone who is having a cardiac emergency.
