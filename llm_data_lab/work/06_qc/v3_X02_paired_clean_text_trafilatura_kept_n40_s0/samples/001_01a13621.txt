I got BabyEbi a small squirty bottle today, he still has trouble shooting in the right direction but it will come with practice. I use soda water - yes, the stuff you usually mix with your drinks, to clean windows and glass. NOTHING makes them sparkle so well, the first time you try it you might have to do it a couple of times to get rid of the chemical build up from previous cleaners, but once that is gone you will be amazed. Just squirt and rub dry with a soft cloth, towelling is good. PLUS it is totally safe to let little ones loose with it, good for the environment too.
