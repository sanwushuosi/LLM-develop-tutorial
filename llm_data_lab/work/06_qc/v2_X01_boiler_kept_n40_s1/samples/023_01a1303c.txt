RUBS is the Ratio Utility Billing System, a method that splits a multifamily property's master-meter utility bill across each unit using factors like occupancy, square footage, bedroom count, and water fixture count. It's the standard alternative to submetering when physical meter installation isn't viable. NES is a turn-key RUBS company that handles allocation, resident billing, AR, and dispute resolution end-to-end.
RUBS is a method of billing multifamily and commercial real estate that distributes utility costs to each resident based on several factors. The combination of factors varies by state and is fully disclosed to residents per state statute. The result: residents pay for their share of usage, and the owner stops absorbing the full master-meter bill.
Submetering is the most accurate form of utility billing because it measures exact usage. But in some properties (pre-1990 construction, slab plumbing, or buildings where there isn't space to install a meter on the incoming water lines) physical submetering isn't possible. In those cases, RUBS is the best solution. It deploys in cycle 1 with no construction, and you can migrate to submetering later on the same NES contract whenever capex is approved.
