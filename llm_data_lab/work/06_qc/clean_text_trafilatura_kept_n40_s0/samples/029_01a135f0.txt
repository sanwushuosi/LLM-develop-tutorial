Congratulatory Memorandum For DFC
Memorandum to Elisha Terrill "Smokey" Stover from H.A. Dozier, Air Officer of USS Yorktown (CV-10) dated 5/18/43 accompanying and congratulating him on being awarded the Distinguished Flying Cross.
Smokey Stover Collection
Elisha Terrill "Smokey" Stover was a F4F Wildcat pilot with VF-8 on the USS Hornet (CV-8) and VF-5 aboard the USS Saratoga (CV-3) and later at Guadalcanal in 1942 where he downed 4 Japanese planes earning the Air Medal.  Transferred aboard the USS Yorktown (CV-10) as a Fighter Director Officer in CIC in 1944, he appeared in scenes of "The Fighting Lady" and later returned to flight status with VF-5 when he was shot down over Truk Atoll on February 16, 1944.
