BULL LAKE SPILLWAY
BULL LAKE SPILLWAY MODIFICATION
An ongoing project overseen by Malcolm International, the Bull Lake Spillway Modification is a project consisting of civil engineering, surveying, and materials testing. FES team members have been present on the job site providing all of these services since it’s initial start in 2018.
LOCATION: Wind River Indian Reservation | Fremont County, WY
PROJECT OWNER: Bureau of Reclamation
MANAGING CONTRACTOR: Malcolm International
SERVICE CATEGORIES: Survey Control | Civil Engineering | Materials Testing
