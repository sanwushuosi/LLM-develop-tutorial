The Nova 3D Whale4 Ultra 16K Resin 3D Printer is a high-resolution LCD resin 3D printer built for users who want exceptional detail, a generous build area, and efficient printing performance. With a 15120 × 6230 resolution, 14 × 19 μm pixel size, and 221 × 118 × 220 mm build volume, the Whale4 Ultra is designed for both detailed models and larger resin printing projects.
Its 16K monochrome LCD provides a high level of detail, while the increased 220 mm build height gives users more freedom when printing taller models. Combined with a rated printing speed of up to 100 mm/h, the Whale4 Ultra offers a strong balance between print quality, size, and productivity.
Key Features of Nova 3D Whale4 Ultra
16K Resolution for Exceptional Detail
The Whale4 Ultra features a 15120 × 6230 resolution display with a 14 × 19 μm pixel size.
This high-resolution printing system is designed to reproduce fine edges, intricate textures, small features, and detailed surface geometry. It makes the printer suitable for users who want smooth and highly detailed resin prints.
For miniature models, prototypes, figurines, decorative parts, and other detailed projects, the 16K resolution provides the level of precision expected from a modern high-resolution resin 3D printer.
Large Build Volume for Bigger Models
With a 221 × 118 × 220 mm build volume, the Nova 3D Whale4 Ultra provides substantial printing space across all three axes.
The 220 mm Z-axis height is particularly useful for taller models and larger components. The additional build space also allows users to arrange multiple smaller parts within the same print.
This makes the Whale4 Ultra a versatile option for both individual large models and batch printing.
Fast Resin 3D Printing
The Whale4 Ultra supports a rated printing speed of up to 100 mm/h.
This gives users the ability to complete suitable projects more efficiently while maintaining the advantages of high-resolution resin printing.
Actual print speed will depend on resin type, layer thickness, exposure settings, model geometry, and slicing parameters.
Advanced COB UV Light Source
The printer uses a 7th-generation COB light source with a light homogenizer and an integrated air-cooling system.
Its optical power intensity is rated at 5000 μW/cm², with a 405nm UV wavelength.
The light homogenizing system helps provide consistent exposure across the printing area. This is important for maintaining reliable curing and consistent detail across different areas of the build platform.
Stable Industrial Motion System
The Whale4 Ultra uses an industrial ball screw and industrial stepper motor for its motion system.
A stable Z-axis is important for resin printing because each layer needs to be positioned accurately during the build process. The industrial motion components help provide controlled vertical movement throughout the print.
Flexible Layer Thickness
The Nova 3D Whale4 Ultra supports layer thicknesses from 20 μm to 200 μm, with 20 μm increments.
